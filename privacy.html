Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026  ·  Just Spanish Academy

Note: This is a provisional version of our Privacy Policy. We are in the process of finalising the full legal document. If you have any questions about how we handle your data in the meantime, please contact us directly at contact@justspanish.com.co.

1. Who we are

Just Spanish Academy ("we", "our", "us") is a boutique Spanish language school offering personalised one-to-one classes online and in person in Bogotá, Colombia. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our students and website visitors.

Contact: contact@justspanish.com.co  ·  (+57) 300 167 2889

2. Information we collect

We may collect the following personal information when you interact with us:

  • Name and contact details (email address, WhatsApp number) provided when you reach out to us or book a session.
  • Information about your Spanish level, learning goals and availability, shared during your intro session or classes.
  • Communication history through WhatsApp, email or other channels you use to contact us.
  • Basic usage data from this website (pages visited, time spent), collected through analytics tools if applicable.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Schedule and deliver your Spanish classes.
  • Personalise your learning experience based on your goals and level.
  • Communicate with you about your classes, progress and any changes to our service.
  • Respond to your enquiries and provide customer support.
  • Improve our teaching methods and the quality of our service.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. How we store your information

Your information is stored securely and only accessed by the Just Spanish Academy team. We retain your data for as long as you are an active student or as required to fulfil the purposes described in this policy.

Communication through WhatsApp is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing their terms at whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your personal data, subject to any legal obligations we may have.
  • Withdraw your consent to our use of your data at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at contact@justspanish.com.co.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website may use basic analytics tools to understand how visitors use our site. No personally identifiable information is collected through cookies without your consent. We will update this section with more detail as our analytics setup is finalised.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
